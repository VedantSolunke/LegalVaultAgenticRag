# BNS-only corpus with IPC mapping

Status: accepted

The first shippable version answers substantive legal questions only against the BNS corpus and treats IPC→BNS mapping as a first-class use case backed by curated mapping data. BNSS, BSA, case law, and secondary sites such as devgan.in are out of corpus: the system refuses or redirects rather than improvising. This keeps retrieval, evaluation, and citations bounded for a small multi-user deployment while still serving lawyers transitioning from IPC numbering.
