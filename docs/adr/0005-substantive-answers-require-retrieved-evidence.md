# Substantive answers require retrieved evidence

Status: accepted

If retrieval and verification do not support a statutory claim, the system refuses substantive legal conclusions and explains what was attempted (e.g. suggest section lookup or more facts). Purely meta or educational questions about the BNS framework (what the act is, how to read a section) may be answered from the model with a clear non-advice disclaimer and without fabricating section text. Fact-pattern, applicability, and IPC mapping answers always ground in retrieved section or mapping records.
